How to use this result

  • Low-risk (0–2): original proposed algorithm supports a watch-and-wait approach / continued active surveillance when clinically appropriate.
  • Intermediate-risk (3–5): Fukuda et al. identified this group as the proposed optimal window for considering lenvatinib initiation.
  • High-risk (6–8): original algorithm supports considering lenvatinib; this group also had the poorest prognosis after treatment initiation in the derivation cohort.
Source: Fukuda N, Toda K, Udagawa S, et al. A proposed clinical scoring system for initiation of lenvatinib treatment in radioiodine-refractory thyroid cancer patients. Endocrine. 2022;76(1):70–77. doi:10.1007/s12020-021-02963-z.

Derivation: single-center retrospective cohort, n=59. This is a proposed clinical decision-support score and should not be used as a stand-alone treatment mandate.
