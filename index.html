TV-DT (years)—
Fukuda TV-DT item—
Quick 2-study estimator adapted from the Radiology Tutor tumour volume doubling-time calculator: TV-DT = Δt × ln(2) / ln(V₂/V₁). In diameter mode, spherical growth is assumed, so V₂/V₁ = (d₂/d₁)³. If the lesion is stable or smaller at follow-up, a positive doubling time cannot be calculated.

Interpretation

  • Low risk (0–2): continued active surveillance may be appropriate when clinically suitable.
  • Intermediate risk (3–5): the original study proposed this range as the optimal window to consider initiating targeted therapy.
  • High risk (6–8): prompt multidisciplinary assessment for targeted therapy initiation is recommended.
Reference: Fukuda N, Toda K, Udagawa S, et al. A proposed clinical scoring system for initiation of lenvatinib treatment in radioiodine-refractory thyroid cancer patients. Endocrine. 2022;76(1):70–77. doi:10.1007/s12020-021-02963-z.
Original derivation: single-center retrospective cohort (n=59). This score is intended as clinical decision support only.
TV-DT calculator reference: Radiology Tutor, Tumour volume doubling time. Two-study formula: TV-DT = Δt × ln(2) / ln(V₂/V₁); diameter mode assumes spherical tumor growth.
